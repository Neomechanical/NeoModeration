# Everyday words in one language that another language's list bans, or that
# read as a banned word with a letter doubled ("slutt"). They pass for everyone
# on a server whose locale is the language named. On a server that sets
# trustPlayerLanguage they also pass for a player whose game language is the
# language named, when the message is written in that language: no English word
# an insult is framed with ("you", "dirty", "get out"), at least one everyday
# word of the language (common-words.txt) that English does not use, and more
# of its words than English ones, leaving out chat and game words such as "lol"
# and "spawn". "ce retard me tue" passes for a French player; "shut up retard
# le" and "dirty kike de" do not. "<language>: <word> <word> ..."; several
# languages may share a line, and a language such as "pt_pt" names one
# country's form, "sv" every Swedish one.
#
# French and Catalan: "en retard", late
fr: retard retards
ca: retard retards
# Spanish: Kike, short for Enrique; "una porra", a betting pool
es: kike porra
# Portuguese in Portugal: "os putos", the kids; "a bicha", the queue
pt_pt: puto putos bicha bichas
# Swedish and Danish: "slut", the end, and "foder", animal feed; Swedish
# "fick", got, and "putta", put
sv: slut fick putta foder
da: slut foder
# Danish and Norwegian: "fag", a school subject; "putte", put; Norwegian
# "slutt", the end, and "kikke", to look
da no nb nn: fag putte
no nb nn: slutt kikke
# Dutch: "huren", to rent
nl: huren
# Croatian, Serbian and Bosnian: "dva puta", twice; Marica, a girl's name
# (a pet form of Marija)
hr sr bs: puta marica
# Italian: "verga", a rod, and the writer Giovanni Verga
it: verga
