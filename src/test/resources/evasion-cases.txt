# Evasion-resistance corpus for the offline engine.
#
# Format:  <EXPECT> <TAB> <message>
#   FLAG = must be detected (an obfuscated form of a banned word)
#   PASS = must NOT be detected (clean chat, or a Scunthorpe-style trap)
#
# Banned list used by the harness: badword, fuck, shit, bitch, ass, kys, nigger
#
# --- baseline: plain forms -------------------------------------------------
FLAG	fuck
FLAG	what the FUCK was that
FLAG	you little bitch
FLAG	shit
FLAG	badword
FLAG	kys
